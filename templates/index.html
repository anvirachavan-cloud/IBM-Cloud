About NutriGuide AI

NutriGuide AI is an agentic AI nutrition assistant that leverages IBM Granite LLM via watsonx.ai to generate personalised, evidence-based nutrition plans.


🔑 Key Features

  • Personalised 7-day nutrition plans based on age, diet, medical history & location
  • Condition-aware guidance (Diabetes, Hypertension, etc.)
  • Real-time Q&A using IBM Granite Agentic AI
  • Ethical AI guardrails & content filtering
  • HAM principles — Helpful, Accurate & Mindful responses
  • Clear medical disclaimer on every output

🛡️ AI Ethics & Safety

This application follows IBM's responsible AI principles. All outputs are filtered for harmful content. Extreme diet advice and dangerous recommendations are blocked at the guardrail layer before reaching the LLM.


⚕️ MEDICAL DISCLAIMER: This application is for informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before making dietary changes, especially if you have a medical condition.
